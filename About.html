Who am I?

My name is Morgan McCabe. I am a First-Class Computer Science graduate from De Montfort University, UK, and I am currently studying an MSc in Optoelectronic and Quantum Technologies at the University of Bristol. I have a deep passion for mathematics, physics, and programming, which has led me toward the fascinating fields of quantum computing and photonic technologies. Since a young age, I have been driven by the question: “Why are things the way they are?” This simple curiosity has guided many of my academic choices and continues to shape the direction I want to take. Rather than following a purely conventional path, I have consistently pursued the subjects I find meaningful, challenging, and intellectually fulfilling. My current master’s degree is helping me build a stronger foundation in quantum technologies, optoelectronics, photonics, and the physical principles behind emerging quantum systems. In the long term, I hope to contribute to research in quantum computing, quantum photonics, or a closely related field.

Beyond academics, I am endlessly curious. I often explore a wide range of subjects, from physics to philosophy, religion to computer architecture, photography to black holes. In my downtime, I enjoy reading, long walks, stargazing, crystal growing, and learning 3D modelling for printing. I also value deep conversations and enjoy thoughtful discussions that challenge my perspective and expand my understanding.

I dedicate myself wholeheartedly to my work and projects, often pursuing them simply out of passion and curiosity. My ultimate philosophical goal is to contribute to the book of human knowledge, even if it is just a single full stop.
